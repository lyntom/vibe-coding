## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, accessible, and dignified civic digital presence tailored specifically for municipal public governance, housing, and urban settlement administration. 

### Design Philosophy
- **Institutional Trust:** Every layout, interaction, and content container projects accountability, statutory precision, and public reliability without bureaucratic opacity.
- **Civic Inclusion & Clarity:** Built for universal access across diverse socioeconomic and technological demographics, prioritizing instant legibility and cognitive ease.
- **Urban Groundedness:** Balances civic formality with community warmth, representing public shelter, infrastructure modernization, and urban environmental welfare.

### Design Movement: Modern Civic Architecture
The style fuses **Corporate / Modern** civic accessibility with clean architectural structuring:
- Flat, well-demarcated surfaces anchored by purposeful contrast lines.
- No gratuitous blurs or skeuomorphic decorations.
- Systematic structural hierarchy inspired by architectural schematics, land-use zoning cartography, and official municipal gazettes.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate institutional identity that aligns with regional governance standards, municipal authority, and public welfare.

### Roles & Semantic Assignment
- **Primary (`#374774` - Navy Slate):** Anchors state credibility, institutional headers, authoritative CTAs, primary navigation surfaces, and high-emphasis typographic headings.
- **Secondary (`#EAB630` - Gold Warm Amber):** Serves as a targeted accent for official verification badges, active states, key application notifications, service highlights, and urgent administrative callouts. It is applied selectively to retain high visual impact without diluting civic seriousness.
- **Tertiary (`#285E61` - Forest Teal):** Represents sustainable urban settlement development, ecological land use, green spatial planning, and positive validation states.
- **Neutral (`#4A5568` - Slate Gray):** Ensures balanced reading contrast across body text, secondary metrics, subtle dividers, and structural scaffolding against crisp administrative surfaces (`#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF`).

### Contrast & Compliance
All primary interactive pairings adhere strictly to WCAG 2.1 AA and AAA standards. Gold Warm Amber is paired exclusively with deep navy or charcoal text when used as a fill to ensure uncompromising contrast.

## Typography

The design system relies on **Public Sans** across all typographic applications. Public Sans is an institutional-grade typeface designed specifically for civic governance interfaces, providing superior legibility in complex administrative forms, data tables, public notices, and mobile civic portals.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Display & Headlines:** Set in weights 600 and 700 with slight negative letter spacing to provide decisive editorial hierarchy for portals, official decree headers, and public announcements.
- **Body Text:** Maintained at regular weight (400) with generous line heights (1.5x) to guarantee effortless readability of lengthy housing policies, regulatory bylaws, and procedural instructions.
- **Labels & Metadata:** Set in medium (500) and semibold (600) with positive letter spacing to establish crystal-clear legibility on document identifiers, status chips, permit codes, and table headers.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured, predictable 12-column layout grid organizes complex civic workflows, application submissions, and geographic housing data.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with `2rem` (`32px`) external margins and `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters. Max content bounding box capped at `1280px` for optimal reading scan lines.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (`24px`) margins and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) margins and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters. Multi-column forms strictly reflow to single-column order.

### Vertical Rhythm
A rigid 8px baseline rhythm aligns input heights, button hit targets, and content panels to ensure consistency across distinct municipal service modules.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve civic credibility and avoid visual clutter, depth is achieved through **low-contrast outlines** paired with **tonal layers** and restrained **ambient shadows**.

### Elevation Scales
1. **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#F8FAFC` base application background.
2. **Level 1 (Surface Containers):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0`. Used for data tables, standard cards, and document modules.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a subtle ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(55, 71, 116, 0.08)`, paired with a 1px border in `#CBD5E1`.
4. **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs / Critical Confirmation Panels):** `#FFFFFF` elevated surface with `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(55, 71, 116, 0.16)`. High focus, blocking interaction below.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`1`)** geometry:
- Standard interactive elements (buttons, inputs, status tags) utilize `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radius.
- Cards, administrative modules, and document containers utilize `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius.
- Large modals, banner containers, and informational drawers utilize `0.75rem` (`12px`) corner radius.

This geometry maintains an architectural, orderly aesthetic appropriate for structural planning, housing documentation, and governmental authority, deliberately avoiding overly playful or consumer-app curvature.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background Navy Slate (`#374774`), text `#FFFFFF`, 4px corner radius, bold/semibold label. Minimum touch target of 44px. Hover state deepens to `#2A3759`.
- **Secondary / Accent Button:** Background Warm Amber (`#EAB630`), text `#1E293B`, 4px corner radius. Used for high-priority civic actions (e.g., "Ajukan Permohonan"). Hover state brightens to `#F5C443`.
- **Outline Button:** Border 1.5px solid `#374774`, text `#374774`, background transparent.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E293B`, roundedness 4px. Focus state triggers a 2px stroke of `#374774` with an adjacent 2px outline in `#EAB630` at 30% opacity. Mandatory civic fields are marked with a distinct crimson indicator (`#DC2626`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Precision-engineered with `#374774` active fills, white glyphs, and a 1.5px inactive stroke in `#94A3B8`. Selection indicator ensures 3:1 contrast against adjacent background colors.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Verified / Approved:** Background `#E6FFFA`, text `#234E52`, border 1px solid `#B2F5EA`.
- **In Review / Pending:** Background `#FEFCBF`, text `#744210`, border 1px solid `#FEEBC8`.
- **Official Notice:** Background `#EBF8FF`, text `#2A4365`, border 1px solid `#BEE3F8`.
- **Urgent / Rejected:** Background `#FFF5F5`, text `#742A2A`, border 1px solid `#FED7D7`.

### Cards & Data Panels
- Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px border in `#E2E8F0`. Header bars in cards optionally incorporate a top 3px accent stroke in Navy Slate or Warm Amber to signify administrative categories (e.g., Housing Verification, Settlement Infrastructure, Public Complaints).

### Specialized Civic Components
- **Public Service Status Tracker:** Horizontal step-progression component with clear numeric rings, current milestone pulse in `#EAB630`, and completed items in `#374774`.
- **Official Document Banner:** Bordered informational notice with the municipal seal placeholder, department identifier, and verified timestamp.
